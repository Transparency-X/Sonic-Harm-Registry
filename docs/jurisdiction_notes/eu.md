# European Union – Acoustic Harm Notes

- **Acoustic surveillance:** governed by ePrivacy Directive and GDPR; requires judicial warrant.
- **Audio monitoring in workplace:** Article 88 GDPR, ICO guidance (UK specific post‑Brexit but influential).
- **Sound harassment:** not a specific crime, but can be prosecuted under general harassment or nuisance laws.
- **Acoustic violence:** WHO limits (55 dBA night, 65 dBA day) are recommended, not binding.
